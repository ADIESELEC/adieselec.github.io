Home EV Charger Installation Costs in Cape Town: What Homeowners & Landlords Need to Know

Why Install a Home EV Charger?

Electric vehicles are becoming a common sight on Cape Town’s roads, and many homeowners in the Metro—whether in Retreat, Constantia, or any other suburb—are looking to install a home EV charger. Having a dedicated charging station brings convenience, saves money on public charging fees, and adds value to your property. It also supports South Africa’s push toward greener transport and helps reduce carbon emissions.

Key Factors That Influence Installation Costs

Several variables affect the total price you might pay for a home EV charger installation. While we can give you a general price range, the final quote depends on an on‑site assessment by a licensed electrician.

1. Charger Type and Power Level

  • Level 1 (120 V) – Basic plug‑in units, cheapest but slowest.
  • Level 2 (240 V) – Most common for homes, offers 7‑22 kW charging speed.
  • DC Fast Charger – High‑power units (50 kW+) are rare for private homes and cost significantly more.

2. Existing Electrical Infrastructure

If your home already has a dedicated circuit and sufficient amperage (usually 40‑50 A), the installation cost will be lower. Older properties may need rewiring or a new service panel, which adds to the expense.

3. Location of the Charger

Placing the charger near your main electrical panel reduces cable runs and labour. Hard‑to‑reach spots or outdoor installations that require extra conduit and weatherproofing will increase the price.

4. Permits and Compliance Checks

Local authorities in Cape Town may require building permits for new electrical installations. Our team at Adie's Electrical Solutions handles all necessary paperwork to keep you compliant.

Typical Cost Range (Starting Point)

For most Cape Town households, a Level 2 charger installation falls roughly between R3,000 and R6,000. This range covers the charger unit, labour, wiring, and any required permits. However, each property is unique, so the final figure can be higher or lower after our electrician assesses your home.

Steps We Follow for a Seamless Installation

  3. Detailed quote with all labour and material costs.
  4. Scheduling of the installation date, including any electrical upgrades needed.

Is It Worth the Investment?

Yes— installing a home EV charger can save you up to 80 % on charging costs compared with public stations, and it adds convenience for daily drivers and rental properties alike. For landlords, a charger can be a standout feature that attracts eco‑conscious tenants and justifies higher rent.
