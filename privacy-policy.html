Privacy Policy

How Adie's Electrical Solutions collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Who we are

Adie's Electrical Solutions ("we", "us", "our") is a licensed electrical contracting business operating in Kenwyn, Cape Town Metro, South Africa. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal information when you visit adieselectrical.co.za, contact us via our booking form, phone, email, or WhatsApp, or engage us for electrical services.

We are committed to protecting your privacy in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

Information we collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, and information collected automatically when you use our website:

  • Contact details you submit through our booking form, WhatsApp, phone, or email — such as your name, phone number, email address, and the electrical service you require.
  • Job details you share with us to provide a quote or complete work — such as your address, description of the issue, and any photos you send.
  • Website usage data collected automatically via Google Analytics (GA4), including pages visited, general location (city/region level), device type, and how you found our site.
  • Cookies used by Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use our site. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

How we use your information

  • To respond to quote requests and bookings
  • To schedule and carry out electrical work you've requested
  • To issue invoices and, where applicable, Certificates of Compliance (COC)
  • To follow up after a job is completed, including requesting feedback or reviews
  • To understand how people use our website, so we can improve it
  • To comply with legal and regulatory obligations

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

Who we share information with

We only share personal information where necessary to run our business, including:

  • Formspree — processes the contact form submissions on our website
  • Google Analytics & Google Tag Manager — helps us understand website traffic (data is aggregated and used for analytics purposes)
  • WhatsApp Business — used to communicate with you if you contact us via WhatsApp, subject to WhatsApp's own privacy policy

We do not share your information with any other third party unless required by law.

How long we keep your information

We keep client and job information for as long as necessary to provide our services, meet legal and tax record-keeping obligations, and maintain a record of completed electrical work and issued Certificates of Compliance. You can request that we delete information we no longer need to keep for legal reasons — see "Your rights" below.

How we protect your information

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against loss, misuse, unauthorised access, and disclosure. This includes using secure, reputable third-party services (such as Formspree and Google) for form handling and analytics, rather than storing sensitive data ourselves wherever possible.

Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Request that we correct inaccurate information
  • Request that we delete your information, where we're not legally required to keep it
  • Object to how we use your information
  • Withdraw consent for us to contact you

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies via Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand how visitors use the site. These cookies do not identify you personally. You can disable cookies at any time through your browser settings, though this may affect how parts of the website function.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "last updated" date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent version.
